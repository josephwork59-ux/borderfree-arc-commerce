Borderfree Commerce on Circle Arc Network

Ideas
BFC is an AI-powered borderfree trade commerce and payroll platform that equip agents to facilitating global trade and work from anywhere with trusted, speed, revocable access, and cost effective border=free settlement.

Key Features & Workflow Four AI Agents:

Researcher: Searches the web, reads, and verifies facts and sources on your target seller.
Trust: Payment credentials are linked with a specific agent identity in the decentralized network.
Guardrail: Define pay limits. Agentic monitoring seller reputation level to change or revoke approval rules at any time.
Settlements: Sent USDC or EURC faster global payouts.
